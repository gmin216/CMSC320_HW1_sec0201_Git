Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git offers tracking, feature isolation, collaboration, and peer reviews. These features are considered essential for software project development.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science could be applied to the medical field. The healthcare industry is surrounded by data so it could be used to predict diagnostics, researched/studied for drug discovery, and create personalized treatment plans according the the patient's data. For example, using and analyzing patient data, a MLM could be trained on electronic patient health records to determine risk score for readmission.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging applies the changes we made to the branch to the current branch by tieing their histories together and rebasing changes the base to keep the history in a linear matter.
